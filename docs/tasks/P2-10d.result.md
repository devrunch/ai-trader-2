# P2-10d Result

Implemented by the manager (Claude) because Codex is out of quota until 2026-12-09.

## What changed
- **Contracts:** `admin.ts` holds `OpsFeedsSchema`, `OpsJobsSchema`, `OpsInstrumentsSchema` and `ZerodhaStatusSchema` (strict).
- **API:**
  - `admin-ops`: `OpsOverview` (application) and `OpsController` at `/api/v1/admin/ops/*`; admin-only, with `CsrfHeaderGuard` on POSTs; an unknown job gives 404.
  - Market-data: `GetFeedOverview`, the `FeedLeadership` port, and `RedisFeedLeadership` (the leader lease plus unexpired subscription leases).
  - Instruments: `ListInstrumentSources` and `InstrumentRepository.listSourceStatus`.
  - `JobScheduler.has()`, `list()` and `nextFire()`.
- **Web:** `features/admin` holds the page with Zerodha, Feeds, Jobs and Instruments tabs, plus the `/admin/zerodha/callback` page.
  - The callback reads the token once, strips it from the URL, posts it same-site, and reports the result as a notice on `/admin`.
  - Feeds poll every 5 s and pause while the tab is hidden.
  - Every view has skeleton, error-with-retry and empty states.
  - `Dialog` now takes `title` and `description`.
