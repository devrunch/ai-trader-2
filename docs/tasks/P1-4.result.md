# P1-4 — Instrument sources

Implements FR-SY-1, FR-SY-2, FR-SY-3, FR-SY-5, FR-MD-1 and R-11. Failure checks cover E6 (source circuit breaker) and the source-date portion of E23 (expired contracts). No persistence, search, websocket or quote implementation is included.

## What changed

- `apps/api/src/modules/instruments/application/ports/instrument-source.ts`: exact source/snapshot/mapping interfaces and DI token.
- `apps/api/src/modules/instruments/infrastructure/sources/index.ts`: source constructors and token exports.
- `.../sources/source-runtime.ts`: shared text transport with 30-second timeout, abort propagation, Kolkata date formatting and independent source breakers.
- `.../sources/kite/kite-csv.ts`: dependency-free RFC 4180 CSV reader and Zod boundary validation.
- `.../sources/kite/kite-mapping.ts`: canonical equities, indices, dated futures, options, synthetic continuous contracts, token encoding and skip/parse-failure accounting.
- `.../sources/kite/kite.source.ts`: public four-exchange fetch with `X-Kite-Version: 3` and one aggregate skip log.
- `.../sources/deriv/deriv-pairs.ts`: fixed 25 forex pairs and four metals.
- `.../sources/deriv/deriv.source.ts`: fresh 29-instrument snapshot without network access.
- `.../sources/nasdaq/nasdaq-parse.ts`: pipe-delimited listing parser, exchange mapping, ETF classification and skip accounting.
- `.../sources/nasdaq/nasdaq.source.ts`: two-file public source and one aggregate skip log.
- `.../sources/source-fixtures.spec-helper.ts`: shared fixture transport and deterministic clock/signal helpers; fixtures were not changed.
- `.../sources/{kite-mapping,deriv.source,nasdaq.source,sources.conformance}.spec.ts`: mapping, conformance, transport, E6 and E23 tests.
- `architecture/features/instruments/README.md`: mapping-rules table, source flowchart and transport/identity behavior.
- `architecture/features/instruments/classes.mmd`, `architecture/generated/{deps.mmd,jscpd.md}`: generated diagrams and reports.

## Deviations from the brief, and why

- The NSE fixture's RELIANCE tick size is 0.1, so its pricescale is 10 under the required formula. The test checks the fixture value and a test-only 0.05 variant yielding 20. The original fixture remains unchanged.
- Added `source-runtime.ts` to share timeout, date and breaker behavior, plus a fixture helper to avoid copied test transport logic. E6 tests are included because AGENTS.md requires circuit breakers for outbound vendor calls.
- Source constructors are exported for later composition; persistence/refresh DI registration remains outside P1-4.
