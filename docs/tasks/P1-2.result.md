## Deviations from the brief, and why

- BullMQ rejects colons in queue names. Queue name `<name>` with BullMQ prefix
  `jobs` produces the required `jobs:<name>` Redis queue namespace.
- Shutdown allows up to five seconds after the ten-second grace to persist
  aborted outcomes and close workers before disconnecting the record client.
- Timeout cancellation is cooperative through AbortSignal, as specified by the
  interface. Callback code must honor its signal to stop external effects.
