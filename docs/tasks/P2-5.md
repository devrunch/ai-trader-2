# Task P2-5: Kite live ticker with self-healing

Read `AGENTS.md` first. It's binding. Do only what this brief asks. Plan § P2-5.

## Goal
Live Kite ticks become forming and closed 1-minute bars with no gaps:
- **Reconnect:** a dropped socket reconnects with backoff and jitter and resubscribes everything.
- **Silent sockets:** a socket that's connected but quiet during market hours is treated as dropped (the watchdog).
- **Backfill:** minutes missed during an outage are backfilled from Kite history and marked repaired.
- **Restart:** after a process restart, the forming bar continues.

The recovery logic lives in a reusable `ResilientFeed`, which Deriv (P2-6) will reuse unchanged.

## SRD
FR-MD-4 (≤ 3 connections × 3,000 instruments), FR-MD-5 (subscribe on demand, 60 s grace), E1 (drop → reconnect, resubscribe, backfill, repair), E2 (silent for 30 s while open → treat as dropped), E3 (expired token → re-mint, no silent fallback), E11, E12, E16 (rebuild after restart), NFR-RB-1.

## ResilientFeed rules
1. **Start:** `connecting` → `live` once the transport is open and every current subscription has been re-sent.
2. **Transport close:** `reconnecting`. Retry with `backoffDelay(attempt, { baseMs: 500, maxMs: 30_000 })`, forever until `stop()`, resetting the attempt counter after 60 s of `live`. On reopen, resubscribe everything, then **gap backfill**: for each subscribed instrument whose session is open or was open during the outage, call `backfill(id, lastTickAt ?? outageStart, now)`. A backfill failure doesn't block live ticks; it's logged and retried on the next nightly run.
3. **Watchdog (E2):** if no tick has arrived for `watchdogMs` while at least one subscribed instrument's market is open (`isOpen`), close the transport with reason `watchdog`, which triggers step 2. When every subscribed market is closed, the watchdog sleeps.
4. **Ref-counting and grace:** each subscription is ref-counted per instrument. The last unsubscribe starts a 60 s grace timer; resubscribing inside the grace cancels it. Only after the grace does the vendor unsubscribe go out.
5. **`stopped`** only after `stop()`. `stale` is used when the transport can't open because of auth (E3): the Kite transport reports `AUTH_EXPIRED`, the feed calls `getAccessToken` (which re-mints) once per backoff cycle, and the state carries the reason.

## Kite specifics
- **Client:** `kite-ticker.client.ts` implements `FeedTransport` over `kiteconnect`'s `KiteTicker`, **with its auto-reconnect disabled**, because `ResilientFeed` owns recovery.
  - Mode `full` for instruments with real volume, `ltp` for indices (no volume).
  - `cumVolume` = `volume_traded`; `at` = `exchange_timestamp` (ms), falling back to `last_trade_time`.
  - `instrument_token` is mapped back to the canonical ID through a reverse map built from the mappings.
- **`SubscriptionManager`:** spreads tokens across at most 3 `KiteTicker` connections of at most 3,000 tokens each, filling one before opening the next. Adding the 9,001st token is rejected with `UNSUPPORTED`, with detail `kite instrument cap reached`.

## LiveBars rules
- **Builders:** one `BarBuilder` per instrument, using that instrument's calendar.
- **Ticks:** each tick → `onTick`. `updated` → `FormingBarStore.set` (throttled to at most 1 write per instrument per 250 ms, always writing the latest). `closed` → `upsert1m(id, closed, 'kite-live')`, then `FormingBarStore.clear` when the new bucket starts.
- **Restart:** on the first tick for an instrument after a restart, call `restore` from `FormingBarStore.get`.
- **Session close:** a `closeDue(now)` sweep every 5 s closes forming bars once their bucket has ended, even when no tick arrives (an illiquid stock at 15:30).
