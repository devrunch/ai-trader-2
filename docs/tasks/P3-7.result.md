## Changes
- **A new `NFO` calendar** for Indian derivatives: it closes at 15:40 from 2026-08-03 (the closing-auction rollout) and at 15:30 before that.
- **`calendarForInstrument(id)`** picks NFO for NSE/BSE futures (`ROOT<month><yyyy>`), options (`ROOT<yymmdd><C|P><strike>`) and continuous (`ROOT1!`), and otherwise the exchange calendar. All instrument-level call sites use it: the live bar builder, the Kite, Deriv and US feeds, reads, quotes, the candle store and live aggregation.
- **Muhurat 2026:** 14:20–15:30 for cash, 14:20–15:40 for derivatives. MCX keeps 18:00–19:00, because there was no MCX data for that day (flagged).
- **The bug this fixes:** the live bar builder dropped every NSE futures tick after 15:30, so 10 minutes of real data were lost each day. Test `keeps NSE futures ticks after 15:30 and drops stock ticks then`: it fails on the old calendar and passes now.

## Not changed (by design)
- **F&O stocks ending at 15:15** need F&O eligibility per stock, from the futures list. Meanwhile the tail-fill guard stops re-asking Kite for those minutes, and the planned nightly data check emails when the data and the calendar disagree.
- **The 2026-12-07 revision** (auction at 15:30–15:40): the nightly data check will flag it once the data changes.
