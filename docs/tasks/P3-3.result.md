# P3-3 + P3-4 Result

Implemented by the manager (Claude) because Codex is out of quota until 2026-12-09. Merged together: the live client's exports have no consumer until the chart exists.

## P3-3: the resilient live client
- **`SeriesSync`:** updates apply in `seq` order (stale ones dropped; a gap is reported, not applied); bars merge by time and the server wins.
  - A snapshot keeps older history only when it overlaps; otherwise it drops it, so the chart never draws a hole.
  - Paged-in history overwrites held bars except the newest, which is still forming.
- **`ServerClock`:** the median of the last 5 samples; `time` sync on connect and every 10 min. Display and staleness only.
- **`LiveClient`:**
  - Our own reconnect with full-jitter backoff (500 ms base, 15 s max); socket.io's built-in reconnection is off.
  - Shared, reference-counted series per symbol and interval.
  - Resubscribes with `afterSeq` after a reconnect, a server `resync`, a seq gap, a tab hidden for 5 s or more coming back, `online`, or a wake from sleep (a 15 s tick arriving 30 s or more late). All triggers in one turn collapse into one resubscribe.
  - Stale guard: 90 s of silence on an `ok` series resubscribes once.
  - Events that arrive before the subscribe ack are held for up to 10 s.
  - `unauthorized` stops all retries.
  - Prices go to `LiveStore` as `price:<instrumentId>`.
- **`connection-store`:** a Zustand store read by `useConnection()`.
- **API:** the gateway answers `time` with `{ now }` (E14).

## P3-4: the chart
- **`ChartAdapter`** (tested against a fake `ChartLike`): converts ms to chart seconds; null volume becomes an empty bar; volume is coloured up or down; theme recolouring; asks for older bars once per oldest bar near the left edge.
- **`createLwcChart`:** Lightweight Charts 5.2.1, candles plus a volume pane, `autoSize`, and axis times in the exchange's zone.
- **`LiveChart`:**
  - Subscribes through `LiveClient` and pages older bars (`before` cursor, 500 per page).
  - E15: switching symbol aborts the previous fetch and drops its late answer.
  - FR-UI-7: a stale reason is shown instead of an empty canvas.
  - Colours come from theme tokens.
- **`ConnectionIndicator`** (FR-UI-8) in the terminal header.
