### Separate uncached and cached benchmarks

Each benchmark constructs a fresh 100,000-instrument index. The uncached test constructs **200 unique queries**, asserts their uniqueness and nonempty results, and executes each exactly once without query-result warmup. Its mixed query forms are `company <n>`, `symbol <n>` and `symbol<n>`, for n from 0 through 199. It includes low-selectivity common-term AND queries and ticker prefixes. The hard uncached p95 threshold remains **100 ms**.

The cached benchmark retains the existing 200-request, nine-query repeated workload, including broad prefixes, typos, aliases, ISIN and empty input. It reports separately as CACHED. Common-term postings prepared during index rebuild are index structures shared by both workloads; the unique workload never reuses a query-result LRU entry.

| Full check run | Uncached p50 (ms) | Uncached p95 (ms) | Cached p50 (ms) | Cached p95 (ms) |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 1 | 5.17 | 18.08 | 0.01 | 0.03 |
| 2 | 5.07 | 16.25 | 0.01 | 0.03 |

Cold-query optimization caches at most 64 common term-prefix score maps while building the index, selected from document frequency (at least 10%, minimum 10 documents, prefixes up to 12 characters). Runtime AND queries intersect MiniSearch term-score postings and preserve native scores. Single-character rankings and default order are also prepared before atomic publication. The bounded query-result LRU remains enabled.

## Deviations from the brief, and why

No requirements omitted. The manager review supersedes the original ranking policy. Popularity is a tier above nonpopular matches; exact and ticker-prefix match quality apply within the tier, with curated order as the tie-break. This satisfies both NIFTY-first for `n` and NIFTY1! in the top three for `nifty`. Venue preference reorders only identical-ticker slots within equal higher tiers, before their type/score ordering, preserving other ticker families' relevance.

Added a scoring helper and native-score parity test to make the uncached performance optimization reviewable. No matching rules, coverage floors, performance thresholds or gate configurations were weakened. Architecture documentation describes the final policy and both benchmark workloads. No commits, merges or pushes were made; the task brief and manager-owned files remain untouched.
