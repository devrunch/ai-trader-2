## Deviations from the brief, and why
- **Matching polls bars instead of subscribing to `md:bars:<id>`.** The durable `paper-match` job (5 s, one runner across instances via the JobScheduler lock) reads the one-minute bars since each order was placed. A missed pub/sub message would be a missed fill; polling re-evaluates history, so a restart or outage catches up at the historical crossing price, and needs no subscriber state. The job holds a subscription lease for each instrument with open orders so the feed keeps producing bars. Cost: up to ~5 s latency; an intrabar spike is caught through the bar's high/low.
- **Square-off runs every minute, not as one 15:20 cron.** Each MIS position stores its square-off time (session close − 10 min, from the calendar), so half days and the Muhurat session need no extra schedule, and a missed run is caught up on the next minute. Square-off bypasses gates and, with no price at all, closes at the average price and logs it.
- **No partial fills.** Each order fills completely once.
- **Option writing and delivery short sales are rejected** (fail closed), since margin for them isn't modelled.
